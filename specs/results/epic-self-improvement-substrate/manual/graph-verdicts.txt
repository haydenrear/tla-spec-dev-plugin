THE PART WORTH KNOWING BEFORE THE NEXT PERSON RUNS THESE.

sktSurface and sktHooks are OPT-IN. The bare command

    python3 test_graph/run-graphs.py

runs three graphs, prints PASSED three times, and reports the other two as
NOT RUN in a line that is easy to read past. The two it skips are the ones
covering skt -- the surface this epic changed repeatedly on 2026-09-24. A green
default-set run is therefore NOT evidence that skt is green. Both must be
named:

    python3 test_graph/run-graphs.py --only sktSurface --only sktHooks

Each graph appears exactly once in the runner's verdict table; the double-print
defect that printed an opt-in graph twice is still fixed.
