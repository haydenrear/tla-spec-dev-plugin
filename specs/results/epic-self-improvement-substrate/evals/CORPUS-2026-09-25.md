## A hypothesis I had and checked

`scaffold-a-program-model`'s three missing markers are all SANY/TLC checks, and
the batch moved the eval `HOME` out of the checkout — so the obvious mechanism
was `verify.sh` failing to find `tla2tools.jar` under `$HOME/.skill-manager`.

**Not supported.** The re-run's `verify.log` reads
`jar=/Users/hayde/.skill-manager/bin/cli/.spec-double-compiler/tla2tools.jar` —
found by absolute path, independent of `HOME`. It was variance.

Recorded because the plausible mechanism would have made a clean story, and the
check took one re-run.

## What this figure is, and is not

**Is:** 64 cases at one commit, one command, in a single run — the coherent
baseline SI-23 needs, taken after the instruments stopped moving.

**Is not:** a stable measurement. Every case is `runs: 1`, and one case was
measured moving ±0.8 between identical invocations. Three of the six below 1.00
here were at 1.00 in the previous corpus run, and seven that failed there are at
1.00 here. SI-23 must either raise `runs` for what it reads or state the limit
in its verdicts.

**Also not:** comparable to anything before `ee237e21`. The corpus was
re-instrumented mid-ladder — eleven instrument defects fixed between the two
runs — so the 78% and the 90% were measured by different instruments. The right
reading is that the earlier number was wrong, not that the substrate improved.
